# FamiliarMac 1.2.1

- Fix a Mission Control crash when opening the overview, including four-finger trackpad gestures. The hidden search field now retains GNOME's native layout allocation.
- Choose whether the Apple F4 / Launchpad / Search key opens Apps or Spotlight in Dock & Window → Mission Control → Shortcuts.
- Press the launcher key again to close Apps or Spotlight, including when opened from the Dock or menu bar.
- Keep mouse button controls and Magic Mouse gestures in Mouse & Touchpad, and simplify the Keyboard & Trackpad window.

Existing shortcut and gesture preferences are preserved. Hardware-specific NVIDIA, Wi-Fi and boot repairs remain excluded from the portable update.

The Mission Control fix was tested in the Zorin VM and confirmed with the physical four-finger gesture. Apps and Spotlight were both tested in the VM. Log out and back in after updating to load the new Shell code.
